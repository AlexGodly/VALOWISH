## Welcome to VALOWISH

**VALOWISH by Alex Godly** is a collection management and planning experience for VALORANT enthusiasts.

Whether you're building a dream Vandal collection, deciding which Ghost skin to buy next, or putting together a complete themed loadout, VALOWISH brings your cosmetics, personal preferences, and purchasing plans together in one place.

Designed with a bold, VALORANT-inspired visual identity, it combines an extensive game catalog with organization tools, personalized rankings, VP planning, and optional account-based cloud synchronization.

## Everything you need to plan your collection

| Experience | What you can do |
|:--|:--|
| **Discover** | Explore weapon skins, bundles, player cards, titles, gun buddies, sprays, Flexes, and game information. Search and filter by category, weapon, edition, and more. |
| **Wishlist** | Save the cosmetics you want, browse them by category, and sort or filter your future collection. |
| **Locker** | Keep track of what you own and view estimated collection value. |
| **Favorites** | Highlight your favorites, with separate views for cosmetics and game intel. |
| **Priority** | Rank purchases globally, by category, or by individual weapon, and calculate estimated VP requirements. |
| **Loadout Studio** | Build multiple personal loadouts and choose weapon skins, gun buddies, and other supported cosmetics through visual pickers. |
| **Buying Simulator** | Create multiple purchase plans, arrange items in buying order, and see running VP totals and budget requirements. |
| **Purchase Planner** | Organize VP goals and track the cosmetics you're working toward. |
| **Insights & History** | Explore your collection statistics and review tracked collection activity. |

### Bundles, in detail

Open a dedicated bundle page to explore its artwork, available information, and your personally tracked contents. You can wishlist, favorite, or mark a bundle as owned, manage individual included cosmetics, and see an **owned-items progress bar** for the contents you've tracked.

**A note on bundle data:** The public cosmetic catalog doesn't reliably provide an official, complete item breakdown for every bundle. VALOWISH supports user-curated contents and keeps suggested matches distinct from verified data. Bundle completion is calculated from the items you've actually tracked.

### Make every VP count

VALOWISH helps turn “I want that skin” into a plan:

1. Add interesting cosmetics to your **Wishlist**.
2. Rank what matters most in **Priority**, including weapon-specific rankings.
3. Arrange potential purchases in the **Buying Simulator**.
4. Compare their estimated costs with your available or planned VP.
5. Record owned items in your **Locker** as your collection grows.

Prices and VP totals are planning tools based on available catalog information and your personal overrides—not a live connection to your in-game wallet or store.

## Built around your collection

**Personal, flexible organization.** Searchable catalogs, category-specific views, edition filters, ordering controls, and multiple saved presets help VALOWISH adapt to the way you collect.

**Designed for desktop and mobile.** A responsive interface keeps browsing, planning, and managing cosmetics practical across different screen sizes.

**Automatic cloud saving.** VALOWISH saves data locally and, when you're signed into a VALOWISH account, synchronizes supported data through Supabase. Sign in with the **same account** on your devices to access your saved collection. Pending or offline changes can sync when connectivity returns; account and connection status still matter.

**Data in your hands.** Use the available backup and recovery tools in Settings for additional protection. Exporting a backup is recommended before major changes or device migrations.

## The project

VALOWISH is a web application developed by **Alex Godly**. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, uses a lightweight HTML/CSS/JavaScript web runtime, retrieves available VALORANT catalog metadata from public sources, and supports optional Supabase-backed account synchronization.

The project continues to evolve with new collection features, planning tools, interface refinements, and compatibility improvements.

Explore the [release history](https://github.com/AlexGodly/VALOWISH/releases) for detailed updates and the [issue tracker](https://github.com/AlexGodly/VALOWISH/issues) to report bugs or suggest improvements.
