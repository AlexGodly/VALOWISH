# VALOWISH v28 — Choose the Plan and What to Add

**Release status:** Experimental (automated logic tests and real-Chromium desktop/mobile tests passed).

## New: "+ BUYING SIMULATOR" opens a chooser
Clicking **+ BUYING SIMULATOR** on any cosmetic (on a card or in the details popup) now asks two things.

**1 · Which plan**
- Every buying plan is listed. The **last used** plan is preselected and marked.
- Plans that already contain the item say so.
- **+ New buying plan** lets you name a new plan on the spot.
- The plan you pick becomes your last used plan for next time.

**2 · What to add**
- **Only this item** (the default)
- **All [Vandal] skins** in the list you're on
- **All [Rifles]** in the list you're on
- **Everything** in the list you're on

"The list you're on" is Discover results, your Wishlist, Favorites, Locker, or the Priority list you're viewing, with your current filters and order. Owned items and items already in the plan are skipped, and the chooser shows how many items and how much VP will be added before you confirm.
